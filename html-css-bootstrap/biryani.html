Chicken Biryani

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • Biryani masala
  • Mint leaves

Cooking Process

  1. Marinate the chicken.
  2. Cook the rice.
  3. Prepare the chicken masala.
  4. Add rice in layers.
  5. Cook for 20 minutes.
  6. Serve hot.
